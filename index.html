I work where hardware and software meet: sensor interfaces, real-time acquisition, low-power wireless links, board bring-up, and the tools that help an engineering team understand a device.

Before Texas A&M, I worked on EV powertrain platform software at Lucid. My earlier experience includes connected products and microcontroller systems at Sanzier IT Solutions.
