POWER / NRF52 / BENCH MEASUREMENT

Build an nRF52 power budget you can verify

List operating states, measure the whole board, and account for the battery boundary.

Technical guide · Dinesh Karnati · October 2026

Budget states

A low-power device spends time acquiring, processing, communicating, and waiting. List the states, their current, and the time each occupies. If states overlap, avoid adding the same current twice.

Average current is the sum of each state current multiplied by its time fraction. Usable battery capacity divided by average battery current gives a first runtime estimate. Use consistent units and specify the operating mode and observation period.

Separate the chip from the board

Nordic’s Online Power Profiler supports an early estimate of radio-related consumption. Its power-budget exercise also accounts for loads outside the SoC. Add sensors, regulators, pull-ups, indicators, and other powered circuits to the board budget.

Measure at the boundary used by the estimate. Current at a regulated rail cannot be compared directly with battery capacity without accounting for voltage conversion and losses. Development-board indicators and a connected debugger can also change the measured configuration.

A hypothetical example

An 8 mA active state occupying 1% of the time contributes 0.08 mA. A 0.01 mA sleep state occupying 99% contributes 0.0099 mA. Add a continuous 0.20 mA board load and the total becomes 0.2899 mA.

These values are illustrative, not nRF52 specifications or wearable measurements. They show why a continuous board load may dominate a short active burst. A battery estimate also needs usable capacity and cutoff behavior rather than only a label capacity.

Verify the budget

Record supply voltage, operating mode, firmware revision, logging configuration, and attached peripherals. Capture long enough to include periodic work, reconnects, and infrequent events. Keep the event structure as well as the average current.

Change one load or condition per comparison. Test the intended battery and regulator configuration before reporting runtime. A credible power claim connects a defined mode to a whole-board trace and a battery run. The calculator provides the starting hypothesis.
